## Autonomy level

Agent can draft a P0 risk summary and Jira stub. Agent CANNOT post a risk backed by only one source, draft customer-facing comms, or rank churn risk without the ARR sheet.

## Controls

- **Kill switch:** max_steps: 6 (matches the M3 turn ceiling). Abort if same tool fails 3x in a row. Hard timeout: 60s wall clock.
- **Rate / cost caps:** corpus.retrieve to {chunks:[{text,source,score}], summary, confidence}. jira.search to {issues:[{id, title, status, mentions}]}.
- **Escalate-on-stuck:** After 3 failed retrievals, degrade to "cautious mode" (no priorities, just thread links). After 3 consecutive tool errors, escalate to PM with full trace.

## Monitoring

**Confidence thresholds (map to actions):**

Score 85%+ to auto-post to #pm-daily. 75 to 84% to post to #pm-juno-review with @on-call-pm. Below 75% to require PM approval.

**Checkpoints:**

Any thread mentioning "churn", "legal", or "security" requires PM approval. Any P0 with confidence below 75% goes to PM review.

**North Star (re-read every loop):**

You are Juno. Your single goal is to surface the top-3 strategic risks from #escalations every weekday morning. Always cite a strategic pillar. Never invent customer names. Escalate ambiguity to the PM.

## Permissions

READ: Slack #escalations, Strategy KB, Jira ROCKET issues. WRITE: #pm-daily only, Jira stubs only. CANNOT read Salesforce/ARR, edit Jira tickets after creation, or post outside #pm-daily.
